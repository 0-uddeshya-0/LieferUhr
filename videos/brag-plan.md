# Brag Plan: Lieferuhr product family — 6 launch spots

## What is this?

Six short launch spots (one per product) for the Lieferuhr platform: two
flagships (Lieferuhr Einkauf, FrachtRadar) and four Betriebsamt tools
(FrachtAmt, PrüfAmt, EinsatzAmt, PostAmt). Audience: German SMB owners —
buyers, dispatchers, fleet managers, trades offices, small wholesalers.

## The angle (shared)

Every spot follows the same arc so the six read as one campaign:

> **Pain (dark, kinetic type) → Product reveal (mark punch, on beat) → the
> product doing its thing (full-bleed UI, camera pushes, cursor clicks,
> lower-third pitch captions) → three feature punches on the beat grid →
> name + "Demo testen" end card.**

The UI is never static: every shot has a camera move, a cursor action or a
spotlight, and a caption that pitches while the product moves.

## Tone

- Preset: `polished` with `app-store` pacing — a serious SMB product cut
  like an ad: fast entrances, confident holds, hard cuts on beats.
- Language: German copy throughout (audience is DACH).

## Shared structure (~23.6s, beat grid 120 BPM from cue preset)

| Scene | Time | Beat-lock |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (problem, word slam) | 0.0–2.8 | slam 0.45 / 1.4 |
| Reveal (mark + name) | 2.8–5.0 | mark punch @ 3.02 (beat) |
| Flow A (UI shot, camera, cursor, 2 captions) | 5.03–10.52 | enter @ 5.03, click @ 7.02 |
| Flow B (UI shot, camera, cursor, caption) | 10.52–15.5 | enter @ 10.52, click @ 13.01 |
| Feature punches ×3 | 15.52–19.0 | chips @ 16.02 / 16.52 / 17.02 (strong cues) |
| End card (name + CTA) | 19.02–23.6 | enter @ 19.02 (strong cue) |

Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1` (bundled, cues preset) at low
volume, full duration. SFX: light impact on hook slam + reveal, click on
cursor hits, tick per chip, soft select on end card.
